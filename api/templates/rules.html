Press Fish Rules

Rules:

1. Log in to your account to start the game.

2. Swim (jump) through the pipes to avoid dying (and to prevent your System32 folder from being deleted).

3. The game consists of 3 levels, with the pipes becoming increasingly difficult in each subsequent level.

4. You earn points for passing through each pipe:
Level 1 – 10 points
Level 2 – 25 points
Level 3 – 75 points

5. At the end of all the levels, there is a boss fight against a shark with 2,500 HP (if you lose, you will be sent back to the beginning of Level 3).

6. You can fire bullets at the shark, with each hit dealing 15 damage. To earn more points, aim for the center of the shark.

7. To win, you must complete all levels and defeat the shark boss.

8. You can die by: touching a pipe, getting hit by the shark's beam, or touching the edge of the screen.

Controls:

Spacebar - Jump

Esc - Pause
